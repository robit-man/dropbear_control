# Iteration 13 discovery plan — real fidelity and runtime integration

Status: `PLANNING-ONLY`

Iteration 13 discovery prepares the evidence that the Iteration 12 runtime
contract intentionally cannot create:

1. independent source and structured graph decisions;
2. reviewed installed-model housing/output/axis CAD;
3. exact-model plant parameter sources and correlation criteria;
4. a pinned ROS 2/plugin build environment;
5. an engine benchmark for closed-chain Dropbear dynamics; and
6. human authorization for visual-only U0 followed by separately reviewed
   isolated adapter work.

Discovery output must identify the decision owner, independent reviewer,
evidence inputs, acceptance metric, expiration/revocation rule and exact
consumer. No plan or benchmark candidate becomes source, graph, CAD, plant,
adapter or physical authority.
