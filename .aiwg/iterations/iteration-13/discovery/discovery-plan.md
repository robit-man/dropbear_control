Status: `PLANNING-ONLY`

fabricate:

3. a pinned rigid-body/ROS execution environment;

inputs, allowed actions, abort conditions, acceptance metrics, expiration,
revocation and consuming gate. Planning or assignment alone grants no source,
graph, applicability, CAD, plant, adapter, physical or motion authority.
