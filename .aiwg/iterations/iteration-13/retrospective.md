- The ROS dependencies and colcon were missing from the target and are now
  explicitly installed and locked.

- The first runner left colcon result logs in the workspace; result logging is
  now redirected to the temporary build root.
- Executable-bit omission on the earlier trace/benchmark runners was captured
  as a real failed machine run before correction.
