## Assignment acceptance checklist

Each assignee must acknowledge:

- exact workload and exclusions;
- independence/conflict status;
- evidence sources and forbidden inferences;
- stop/abort authority;
- expected decision or evidence format;
- due date and review window; and
- replacement/escalation path.

Until source, graph, U0 and custody rows are assigned as required, no authority
registry can become active and no physical campaign can begin.
