## Honest verdict

The bounded **P0–P1 offline implementation exit passes**. WP-020, WP-030 and
WP-130 have green foundations alongside WP-050, Python/native WP-060, WP-070,
the bounded fake-scheduler portion of WP-080, the host-session portion of
WP-110 and the WP-180 repository entry point. The steel thread proves typed
offline composition on a synthetic tuple, but it is not physical
authorization or mechanical observation. Formal stakeholder/reviewer approval
remains open. G0 physical, real transport/retry/bus-off, G2/HIL, physical
firmware applicability, model-complete CAD articulation, actuator plant
fidelity and rigid-body robot simulation remain explicitly unsupported.
